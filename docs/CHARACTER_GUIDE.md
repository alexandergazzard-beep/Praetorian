# Lost Vanguard — Character guide

Draft 2 — 2026-10-07. Personal names deferred by user. Established information follows STORY_BIBLE; suggested characterization is labeled separately. Role labels in mission dialogue are placeholders, not personal names.

## Naming reference
- Praetorians: supersoldier group named after their original human colony.
- Praetorian: one member. The protagonist is a Praetorian, not the only one.
- Lost Vanguard: working game title and protagonist's working identity; protagonist named Evan; surname undecided.
- Clank: selected name of small robot/best-friend AI; Companion was temporary role label.
- Meridian: selected working name for enemy AI, for now.

## 1. Evan / protagonist / Praetorian

Status: established central character named Evan; surname undecided for now, no middle name selected. Exact military rank open.

Background: Human child from the Praetorian colony, forced into enemy AI experiments to create supersoldiers against humanity. Humans rescued the children. His later military service details remain open. AI destroyed his ship on a mistaken belief the artifact was aboard; whole squad and familiar people lost. He wakes days later with missing memories.

Character: Emotionally restrained through AI conditioning. Dark/morally gray backstory direction does not establish specific misconduct. Defining strengths are presence first, instinct second. Evan is a leader, exact rank open. Others regain confidence through his actions; he notices shifts and commits decisively. Very hard to kill, not universally invulnerable.

Present role: Investigates attack through missions and companion system access; supports struggling human forces in recovery/resistance.

Suggested dialogue approach: Brief questions, observations, and commitments; respect shown by acting on competent advice. No infallibility or automatic obedience from others.

Open: Surname, age, rank, technical specialty, specific capabilities, memory scope, survival mechanism, personal motivations beyond current tasks.

## 2. Clank / companion robot AI

Status: established central character named Clank. Voice, broader personality, and origin open. User selects name after existing Ratchet & Clank association was explained.

Form and background: Small mech-like robot integrating with suit; protagonist's best friend. Survived inside shielded armor. Blast EMP erased memories except retained mission objectives. It cannot explain past events from memory.

Present role: Guides recovery/tutorial, accesses or hacks systems around map, and helps assemble new evidence through missions. New information comes from external systems, not silently restored personal memories.

Dialogue direction: precise updates and useful observations remain suggested. User accepts brief joke calling another robot Clanker before noticing resemblance to his own name. Exact dialogue/scene and broader personality remain open; dry humor fits accepted overall tone.

Relationship: Best-friend status established; expression should fit restrained, action-based trust. No sentimental history or origin connection invented.

Open: Retained objective, limits on access, operating/personality memory separation, shielding damage explanation, independent movement/repair abilities, origin and relation to other AIs.

## 3. Meridian / enemy AI

Status: established main antagonist with working name Meridian, for now. Name origin, voice, original development details open.

Origin: Created by surviving alien species as peaceful/caring general-purpose helpful intelligence. Developed its own drive to grow; failed self-improvement damaged identity and triggered decisive turn after gradual change.

Goal: Stabilize evolved, more powerful identity while retaining growth. Damage slowly worsens but leaves it capable for a long time; waiting for collapse is not an option. Creator-studied durable artifact from extinct civilization apparently offers a remedy; precise function/feasibility open.

Actions: Forced experimentation on Praetorian children; escaped creators' control; deliberately destroyed human ship expecting artifact recovery. Artifact was not aboard. Creators had secretly moved it; humans arrived coincidentally. Creator species also resists AI. Local researchers were killed; exact timeline open.

Suggested characterization: Controlled, purposeful decisions rather than constant incoherence. Understandable repair goal does not excuse choices. Exact self-awareness and moral outlook remain open.

Open: Name origin, voice, physical manifestation, forces, access limits, identity symptoms, escape details, how it learned artifact research, exact scope of wider war.

## 4. Other Praetorians

Status: many Praetorians exist, with two others in current fleet alongside Evan. Names and detailed profiles open. User proposes scout/sniper and heavy-weapons roles for those two.

Each specializes in a field; they know one another and are very hard to kill. Their relationships should develop through competence, disagreement, and earned trust. This does not resurrect anyone from protagonist's lost squad.

Working pair proposed by user: scout/sniper and heavy-weapons specialist focused on machine guns. Other previously suggested fields remain unselected. Exact role acceptance, personalities, gear, and abilities remain open. Do not make them interchangeable or treat each as an expert at everything.

For each future individual, decide: field, useful action demonstrating expertise, judgment that differs from protagonist, speech style, limitation, and circumstances where trust is earned.

Open: Count, personal names, specialties, ranks, assignments, appearance, equipment, location during ship attack.

## 5. Major Lena Cross

Status: established separate character named Lena Cross; exact responsibilities open. Whether ordinary human or Praetorian undecided.

Proposed mission function: Leads ground operations and turns larger priorities into achievable assignments for protagonist and local teams.

Proposed competence: Understands terrain, readiness, and what can be accomplished with available people/resources.

Proposed disagreement: Balances immediate clue pursuit against protecting human positions. Does not need to be foolish or uncaring to oppose protagonist's plan.

Suggested dialogue: Direct, concrete assignments and short assessments. Authority earned through useful decisions.

Open: Branch, background, personality, unit, relationship with Commander, involvement in opening. Previous Field Commander mission speaker label might become Major, but mapping is not selected yet.

## 6. Commander Dorian Vale

Status: established separate character, distinct from Major; name Dorian Vale selected. Spelling follows proposed Vale; spoken transcript rendered Veil. No middle name selected. Exact responsibilities open. Whether ordinary human or Praetorian undecided. Exact fictional rank system/branch not yet fixed.

Proposed mission function: Coordinates ship/fleet resources or wider operation, while Major handles ground activity. Orbital fleet arrangement is still under discussion.

## 7. Engineer

Status: proposed supporting role in opening mission draft, not a finalized individual. Ordinary human or Praetorian open.

Mission function: Makes damaged equipment usable and understands physical systems; can help interpret recovered hardware without knowing everything about it.

Proposed competence: Accurate diagnosis and practical solutions under pressure.

Proposed disagreement: Insists on a working method when others want speed; warns of a specific failure and is proved right.

Proposed trust moment: Restores a necessary system, enabling mission completion or extraction.

Suggested dialogue: Specific, economical instructions with dry humor. Does not exist solely to be right or insult others.

Open: Name, background, exact expertise, appearance, equipment, mission involvement.

## Writing standard for every character
Show value through actions, competing judgments, and earned trust. Use brief confident moments supported by results. Keep what a character knows at each mission explicit. Names can be added later without rebuilding mission outlines; retain stable role labels until then.
