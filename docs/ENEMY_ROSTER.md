# Enemy roster — selected starting enemies

Created: 2026-10-08 (America/Los_Angeles).

Updated: 2026-10-08 (America/Los_Angeles). User selects scout drones, line robots and alien skirmishers as the starting enemy set. Meridian enslaves members of its creator species and many other species for use as soldiers. Visible collars and the weapons issued to them should make their captivity apparent. Specific species anatomy, final designs, names and gameplay mechanics remain open.

## Story fit

Meridian is the enslaving AI, rather than its creators enslaving themselves. Some members of the creator species are forced into Meridian's army while others continue resisting it. Meridian also enslaves many different species for its wars. Enemy allegiance therefore does not imply willing loyalty or hostility from every member of a species.

Collars and issued weapons are accepted visual cues of enslavement. Their exact functions and enforcement mechanisms remain undecided: no explosive collar, pain system, mind control, weapon lock or remote control of Evan is established. Collar destruction, liberation, surrender and nonlethal gameplay are open design questions, not requirements. Opening enemies already speak an alien language; a mixed alien/robot search patrol could fit, but its composition and specific species remain open.

## Visual cohesion

Updated direction (2026-10-08): user rejects E1's humanlike robot and alien silhouettes. Explore nonhumanoid machines and several distinct alien species with varied body plans and sizes; human height is not a constraint. E1 is preserved as superseded exploration, not a production appearance reference. E2 supplies new review candidates; no individual species design or size accepted yet. Earlier upright line-robot and lean humanoid skirmisher descriptions above are superseded appearance proposals; selected combat roles remain.

Accepted requirement: alien soldiers visibly wear collars and use weapons that communicate they are being used by Meridian. Apply this across enslaved species while retaining distinct biological silhouettes. Exact collar and weapon appearance remains open.

Proposed implementation: an obvious rigid collar and visibly standardized Meridian weapon hardware, adapted to different hands and bodies. Matching industrial fittings on collars and weapons could visually connect the captives to the robot army. These details are proposals; no control function is implied.

Proposed Meridian machines: dark ceramic armor, pale metallic frame, one consistent sensor/light color, restrained mechanical detail. Palette remains unapproved. Distinguish roles through shape and motion, not color alone.

Recommend introducing roles separately before combining them, and deferring shields, complex quadruped movement and support behavior until the basic enemies feel good.
