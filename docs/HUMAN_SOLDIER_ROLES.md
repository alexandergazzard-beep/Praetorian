# Human soldier roles - 2026-10-08

## User-established direction

Regular infantry uses basic science-fiction military uniforms and is smaller than a Praetorian (D101). User now requests another soldier type analogous in battlefield appeal to Halo ODSTs, somewhat stronger than regular marines, plus ideas for other soldiers. Specific name, armor, insertion method, enhancements and roles remain open. Stronger need not mean biologically enhanced.

## Proposed elite tier

Working name options: Vanguard Rangers, Strike Infantry, or Pathfinders. None selected. Vanguard Rangers is the current suggestion, not a canon military organization.

Conventional elite humans with better training, protected helmets, more complete armor and better equipment. Same normal human height range as regular troops; somewhat bulkier silhouette, substantially smaller than Praetorians. Their appeal comes from discipline and dangerous assignments, not competing with Praetorian enhancements. Breaching ships, securing landing zones and raids are possible assignments; orbital insertion/drop pods are not established.

H2 visual proposal shows Assault, Recon, Breacher and Heavy Gunner variations of one armor family. Charcoal plates/olive fabric, enclosed dual-eye-lens helmet and offset chest fastening. Exact palette/construction and equipment unselected. Proposed hierarchy: regular infantry -> elite infantry -> Praetorians; this is a battlefield capability comparison, not formal rank or command authority.

## Other proposed soldier roles

| Role | Visual cue | Battlefield purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Line marine | Basic plates, open-face helmet, rifle | Main infantry and human scale baseline |
| Assault ranger | Enclosed helmet, fuller articulated plates | Elite squad core for difficult objectives |
| Recon scout | Light armor, helmet sensor, small pack | Observe enemies and find safe routes |
| Breacher | Reinforced forearms/chest, shotgun, compact tool | Clear ship corridors and fortified entrances |
| Heavy gunner | Ammunition webbing, squad machine gun | Suppression and covering movement |
| Anti-armor specialist | Launcher and small missile carrier | Counter vehicles, heavy robots or emplacements |
| Combat engineer | Tool harness, cutter, utility pack | Restore systems, open routes, establish defenses |
| Field medic | Medical pouch and clear original medical marking | Stabilize and evacuate wounded soldiers |
| Signals specialist | Antenna/radio pack, wrist terminal | Restore communications and coordinate squads |
| Drone operator | Controller and compact drone case | Local observation and remote reconnaissance |
| Ship security | Compact weapon, restrained armor, security marking | Defend ship interiors and military facilities |
| Hazard specialist | Sealed protective suit and filtration hardware | Work around smoke, chemical leaks and hazardous wrecks |

These are proposals, not mandatory enemy/NPC classes or new gameplay features. Roles can belong to regular or elite units rather than each creating a new military tier. Shields, invisibility, jump packs, revival mechanics and automatic drone combat are not implied.

## Practical starting roster proposal

Start with regular marine plus elite assault soldier, then recon, breacher, heavy gunner, medic and engineer equipment variations. Reuse two underlying uniform/armor families with interchangeable helmets, packs, pouches and weapons to limit modeling and rigging work. This is production advice; no models or gameplay have been implemented.
