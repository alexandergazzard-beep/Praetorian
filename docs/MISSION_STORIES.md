# Lost Vanguard — Opening mission stories

Draft 1 — 2026-10-07. Narrative/design document only; no Unreal missions implemented or paid voices generated.

## Status and scope
The established opening and story facts come from STORY_BIBLE version 31. Everything extending beyond them below is a proposed five-mission opening arc, not newly accepted canon. Mission titles, locations, human contact arrangement, exact discoveries, dialogue, and support characters are provisional. This covers the opening investigation, not the whole campaign.

Supporting voices use role labels until characters are selected: Evan, Clank, Field Commander, Engineer. Evan/Clank names now selected; officer-role mapping and remaining character names unresolved. Names and exact cast are undecided. No surviving member of the lost squad is introduced. Human support is local in this draft, leaving orbital support/later backup open. Companion system access yields new evidence; its personal memories remain lost.

## 1. Wreckage

### Story
Days after the AI destroyed his ship, the Praetorian wakes beneath debris. His companion guides a recovery test. Alien-speaking searchers enter, looking for survivors and technology; he takes out the party. The proposed continuation is a search for an exit and contact with human forces. A damaged search unit or terminal contains instructions naming a specific artifact. He does not yet know why the AI believes it was aboard.

### Player objectives and scene beats
1. Lift debris and complete look/move/crouch/traverse recovery actions.
2. Detect the approaching search party and survive first combat; weapon acquisition remains a design decision.
3. Let the companion access the search instructions through a short interaction.
4. Reach a functioning ship communication point and receive a nearby human distress transmission.

### Discovery and next mission
Enemy search instructions establish that the wreck search has a specific target. Distress transmission supplies a location and urgent reason to leave for mission 2. Companion retained mission objective remains undecided; current navigation comes from the new transmission.

### Draft voice lines
- Clank: "Recovery check. Look at the light."
- Evan: "How long?"
- Clank: "Several days." [Provisional wording; exact interval undecided.]
- Clank, after accessing search data: "One artifact. They have an entire search plan for it."
- Evan: "Then we find out why."
- Human transmission: "Any human unit receiving: our position is failing. Respond."

## 2. Rally Point

### Story
He reaches a damaged human position. The survivors are fighting an enemy whose reason for attacking remains unknown. His presence steadies their response, but he earns authority through useful action. A field commander directs the defense; an engineer handles recovery/extraction infrastructure. These roles are proposals. The Praetorian helps stabilize the position while the companion shares the wreck search data.

### Player objectives and scene beats
1. Reach the survivors and coordinate with the commander.
2. Restore a route to cover or a defensive power supply with the engineer.
3. Defend the position long enough to relocate exposed survivors.
4. Deliver recovered data and receive the next assignment.

### Discovery and next mission
Human observations suggest an enemy relay coordinates local search forces. Disrupting it could reduce pressure on survivors and reveal what the AI is seeking. Commander assigns relay investigation in mission 3.

### Draft voice lines
- Field Commander: "Praetorian. East passage. We need it open."
- Evan: "On it."
- Engineer: "That panel's dead. Use the lower feed."
- Clank, after attempting a scan: "Lower feed is live."
- Engineer: "Imagine that."
- Field Commander: "Their relay is directing the search. Take its records. Then shut it down."

## 3. Search Pattern

### Story
The relay mission serves a practical military need while advancing the mystery. The companion retrieves targeting records before the player disrupts the relay. These show the human ships were attacked on an assumption of artifact possession, not confirmed identification. This is new evidence rather than recovered companion memory.

### Player objectives and scene beats
1. Reach the relay through a defended route.
2. Secure the access point and protect companion access.
3. Recover targeting/search records before disabling the relay.
4. Leave with coordinates pointing to a prior research location.

### Discovery and next mission
Records link the AI's artifact search to an alien research site in the extinct civilization's remnants. They expose suspected possession, not the actual relocation. Mission 4 investigates the source of that assumption.

### Draft voice lines
- Clank: "I found the targeting basis."
- Evan: "Read it."
- Clank: "Possible artifact transfer. Human vessels entering search area."
- Evan: "Possible."
- Field Commander: "That's their justification. We still need the facts."
- Clank: "The source coordinates are a research station."

## 4. Empty Archive

### Story
The team reaches a research station used by members of the AI's creator species within older alien structures. Its records show researchers moved the artifact when they learned the AI intended to seize it. The AI did not know of the relocation. Local researcher deaths can be evidenced here, but exact circumstances remain open. The creator species survives and is itself resisting the AI.

### Player objectives and scene beats
1. Explore old civilization structures and the newer research installation.
2. Restore access to the station archive; exact interaction design remains open.
3. Retrieve relocation records while surviving a renewed enemy search.
4. Recover a lead to the artifact's transport route or departure facility.

### Discovery and next mission
Relocation records establish the artifact was removed before human arrival and was never aboard the protagonist's ship. The enemy attacked on a mistaken assumption. A partial route record leads to mission 5. This does not settle human/creator prior contact or require a live alien meeting.

### Draft voice lines
- Clank: "The relocation order predates our arrival."
- Evan: "It wasn't on the ship."
- Clank: "No. They moved it before we entered the area."
- Field Commander: "Then the AI is searching the wrong trail."
- Evan: "Let's find the right one."

## 5. Departure Record

### Story
The humans shift from investigating their attack to pursuing the artifact's real route. The Praetorian reaches the departure facility before enemy searchers erase or seize its remaining records. The companion assembles a usable route from damaged records; an engineer can advise on the physical systems. Recovery becomes a race for information, not immediate artifact acquisition.

### Player objectives and scene beats
1. Secure access to the departure facility.
2. Restore the departure recorder and defend the interaction point.
3. Retrieve a viable route lead and withdraw or hold for support.
4. Report the next destination to human command.

### Discovery and next mission
A destination lead opens the next campaign arc. Exact destination, whether relocation was local or off-planet, transport means, and support arrangement remain undecided. The draft ends here rather than invent the final artifact location or ending.

### Draft voice lines
- Engineer: "Recorder's intact. Its power supply isn't."
- Evan: "Tell me what you need."
- Clank: "Route recovered. Destination is incomplete."
- Field Commander: "Enough to follow?"
- Clank: "Enough to start."
- Evan: "Then we move."

## Narrative-to-level handoff
For each approved mission, expand this draft into: opening scene, required objectives, optional exploration, interactions and combat beats, voice-line triggers, discoveries, exit condition, and transition to the next mission. Keep information revealed by logs separate from knowledge characters already possess. Mandatory clues must appear on the required route so the next assignment never depends on an optional collectible.

Do not build the campaign automatically from this document. First review the arc, approve or revise its new details, then develop mission 1 into a complete scene-and-dialogue script. Future Unreal implementation should match that approved script, with play tests confirming objective progression and line timing.
