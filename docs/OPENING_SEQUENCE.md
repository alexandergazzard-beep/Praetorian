# Opening sequence — Lost Vanguard

Updated 2026-10-07. This is a story/design record, not an implemented level.

## Established by the user

Days after the ship's destruction, the Vanguard wakes inside its wreck, pinned under debris. He pushes the wreckage off himself. His small robot companion guides a cognitive/recovery test that teaches the player movement. Afterward, he hears enemies speaking an alien language as they search the wreck for survivors and technology. He takes them out.

The blast produced an EMP. The robot survived inside the Vanguard's shielded suit but lost its memories apart from retained mission objectives. Neither companion nor protagonist has a complete explanation of what happened.

This replaces the earlier opening inside an abandoned alien fortress. The AI destroyed the ship in the current draft; deliberate sacrifice is superseded for now. The established squad and crew loss remains, with no additional surviving squad members introduced.

## Suggested staging, not canon

1. Wake with limited sound and suit readouts. An interaction lifts the debris, demonstrating strength.
2. Robot asks the player to look toward a light, move a few steps, crouch under a collapsed support, and cross a short gap. Its practical recovery instructions introduce controls.
3. Suit identifies the elapsed time. The delay surprises both of them; exact interval remains undecided.
4. Alien voices interrupt. The robot lowers its voice; the tutorial transitions into a live encounter.
5. The player obtains a weapon or uses an available one. This detail is undecided.
6. After the encounter, a surviving mission objective creates direction without explaining the attack. The objective itself needs user selection.

Possible dialogue (draft only):
- Robot: "You're awake. Stay with me. Look at the light."
- Vanguard: "Where's everyone?"
- Robot: "I have the mission objectives. The rest of my records aren't responding."

## Consistency questions

- Protect core operating routines/personality separately from damaged episodic memories? Proposed explanation for the robot's ability to teach the tutorial.
- Why did suit shielding preserve the robot's hardware but not all memory? Possibilities include incomplete shielding or memory damage during the power failure. None selected.
- How did the Vanguard survive the blast and remain alive for several days? Emergency suit life support/stasis is a possibility, not established.
- What caused the Vanguard's own memory gap? EMP is not automatically an explanation for biological amnesia.
- Where is the wreck and why are scavengers reaching this section after a week?
- What mission objective survived, and what does it hide or reveal?

The previous assistant's ancient-weapon/mobile-power-source scenario remains an unaccepted proposal.
