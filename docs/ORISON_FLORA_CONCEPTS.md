All six are visible and individually numbered. Blue-green foliage, violet accents and related ribbed/forked leaves give a coherent visual family. The mature tree has much denser leaves than the understory specimens. Soil, stones and incidental small plants appear at the bases; those are presentation dressing and should be excluded from standalone plant references unless explicitly chosen. Plants are framed individually, not at a shared scale.

This sheet is concept art, not optimized game geometry. Select designs first, then create one isolated reference per chosen plant before modeling. Keep trunks/branches distinct from reusable foliage; evaluate actual geometry, material and scene cost during asset production.
