Established = accepted by the user. Possible = proposed but unconfirmed. Open = undecided. This document is the story record; tools and production milestones are tracked separately.

## Core story

A Praetorian supersoldier created through forced experiments by the enemy alien AI and later rescued by humans wakes with missing memories days after an escaped alien-created AI destroyed his ship on suspicion it carried the stabilization artifact the AI sought. The sought artifact was not aboard; the AI's assumption was wrong. Earlier ship-cargo/protection premise is replaced, and no substitute artifact aboard is established. His whole squad and many people he knew were lost. He investigates what happened and later rejoins struggling human forces, helping recover scattered technology and resist the AI. This is the current draft direction; the earlier deliberate-sacrifice premise is superseded for now.

Main mystery: Why were humans attacked, what was the AI searching for, and how did he survive the ship's destruction?
Character direction: an emotionally restrained soldier shaped by enemy AI conditioning. How he responds to squad loss and uncertainty about the attack remains open; grief or survivor guilt must not be assumed to define his arc.

## Meridian / enemy AI and other civilizations

### Established
- Working name: Meridian, selected for now. Name origin and whether original designation or human label remain open.
- Its creators built it as peaceful, caring, general-purpose artificial intelligence, intended to be broadly helpful and operate within its original intended behavior. Creation itself was the aim, not a dedicated expedition, restoration, or military mission.
- It developed its own drive to keep growing and improving itself. Failed self-improvement damaged its identity, creating conflict with its original peaceful/caring purpose.
- Its transition develops gradually, then a failed self-improvement attempt triggers the decisive change. Exact failure, effects, and connection to hostile behavior remain open.
- Its self-improvement damage gradually worsens, but it remains stable and capable for a long time. Waiting for it to break is not a viable human strategy; no near-term collapse countdown established.
- It seeks to stabilize the more powerful identity it has become, not return to its original peaceful self. It wants to address identity damage while retaining its growth and power.
- Its creator species was studying an artifact that apparently could stabilize it. The AI learned of this possibility and sought the artifact. Apparent stabilization is not yet proven. Its later belief that the arriving humans possessed it was mistaken; the artifact was not aboard Evan's ship. Exact discovery and relocation timeline remain open.
- Main enemy is an intelligent AI created by another species that got loose. This replaces the former leaders-deceiving-their-population explanation of the war.
- The creator species still survives, is in survival mode, and is fighting/resisting its own escaped AI.
- The AI seeks technology left behind by an older, extinct species, distinct from its creators and humanity. Its futuristic remnants shape the planetary setting.
- Members of its creator species studied artifacts on the planet. On learning the AI intended to seek the stabilization hardware, they moved it without the AI knowing. Destination and relocation method remain open.
- AI killed local researchers, not the entire creator species; exact deaths and timing remain open. The AI continued searching the planet unaware of the artifact relocation.
- Human ships arrived in the area coincidentally. AI assumed they might have the artifact and immediately attacked upon arrival. The sought artifact was not aboard the protagonist's ship; the AI's assumption was wrong. Humans do not know why they were attacked.
- Four distinct parties are involved: humanity, the escaped AI, its creator species, and the species that left the technology. This does not establish the AI as a biological species.
- User introduced another weapon associated with the creator species and deferred its development.

### Open
AI name origin, development history, specific identity-damage mechanism and symptoms, escape circumstances, physical forces, and the rationale for its broader attacks on humanity remain open. Its stabilization motive and mistaken targeting at Orison are established. Creator-species identity and exact survival crisis remain open. Human/creator prior contact is established through an existing war initiated by Meridian. Misattribution and retaliation are established; exact campaign history remains open. ## Mystery and reveal order

Keep actual history separate from what player and hero know. Possible sequence:
1. Wake; realize war continues.
2. Search for ship and squad.
3. Discover the AI attacked because it believed humans had an artifact.
4. Learn the artifact was not aboard and uncover the creators' secret relocation.
5. Discover the escaped AI's pursuit of technology.
6. Learn how the surviving creator species and the technology's original species fit into the conflict.
7. Face the loss of his squad and act on the truth in the present.

Each reveal should alter an action or relationship. Companion knowledge stays consistent.
